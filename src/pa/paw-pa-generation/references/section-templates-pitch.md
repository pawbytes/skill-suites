# Section Templates — Pitch

Type: `pitch` — pre-sale persuasion to win the deal. Tone: confident, outcome-led, respectful of the client's time. The client should feel understood in paragraph one and excited to talk by the end.

## Section order

1. Assumptions callout (autonomous only)
2. **Opening hook** — client's problem → your specific outcome promise (2–3 sentences max)
3. **Why now** — urgency or cost-of-inaction from research intel (optional, 1 short paragraph)
4. **Our understanding** — mirror brief requirements back; prove you listened
5. **Recommended approach** — methodology in 3–5 bullets; tie each to a requirement
6. **Proof** — 2–3 case studies (local matches prioritized); outcome metrics
7. **What you get** — deliverables summary (from scope templates); keep scannable
8. **Investment** — tiered pricing preferred; anchor on recommended tier; value rationale
9. **Timeline** — high-level phases; one paragraph or simple table
10. **Risks we'll manage** — top 3 risks only (compact table)
11. **Why us** — team bios snippet + about-us boilerplate (short)
12. **Terms** — verbatim from `terms.md` (appropriate variant)
13. **Next step** — single clear CTA (call, workshop, signed SOW)

## Tone notes

- Lead with **their** problem, not your company history.
- Use client language from the brief and research dossier.
- Pricing: present tiers as choices, recommend one with rationale ("most clients in your situation choose…").
- Keep total length scannable — aim for 3–6 pages in the standard draft.
- Objection weaving: budget → phased option in investment; timeline → timeline section; fit → proof section.

## Template skeleton

```markdown
# {Proposal title — outcome-oriented}

> **Assumptions** *(autonomous mode only)*
> - {assumption 1}
> - {assumption 2}

## {Client name} — {one-line outcome promise}

{Hook: 2–3 sentences on their problem and your angle. Client's words.}

### Why this matters now
{Optional urgency paragraph from research intel.}

## Our understanding
{Mirror requirements and constraints from brief.}

## Recommended approach
1. **{Phase/activity}** — {maps to requirement}
2. **{Phase/activity}** — {maps to requirement}
3. **{Phase/activity}** — {maps to requirement}

## Relevant experience
### {Case study 1 — local match}
**Challenge:** … | **Outcome:** … | **Relevance:** …

### {Case study 2}
…

## What you'll receive
- {Deliverable 1}
- {Deliverable 2}
- {Deliverable 3}

## Investment
{Tiered pricing table or value-anchored single price}

*{Calibration/sanity-check rationale — 2 sentences max.}*

## Timeline
| Phase | Duration | Key output |
|-------|----------|------------|
| … | … | … |

## Risks we'll manage
| Risk | Likelihood | Impact | Mitigation |
|------|------------|--------|------------|
| … | … | … | … |

## About {seller name}
{About-us boilerplate — abbreviated}

### Your team
{Bio snippets from bios.md}

## Terms & conditions
{Verbatim from brand/boilerplate/terms.md — selected variant}

## Next step
{Single CTA — specific date/action.}
```

## Short variation (`draft-v1-short.md`)

Sections: Assumptions (if any) → Hook → One proof point → Investment summary → CTA. ~400–600 words.

## Long variation (`draft-v1-long.md`)

Add: expanded case studies (all matches), full deliverable detail with acceptance hints, complete risk register (5+ rows), expanded timeline with dependencies, discount scenario note if present.
